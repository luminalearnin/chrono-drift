# Reference research: Archery Bird Hunter

The reference is **Archery bird hunter** by KohistaniApps. Its public Google Play listing describes a two-dimensional arcade archery game with six published scenarios:

1. A tree and dog, with birds perched on branches; the dog collects defeated targets in a basket.
2. A river scene with flying ducks. If the player waits roughly 5–10 seconds, ducks settle on the water and become easier targets.
3. A forest scene where birds are followed from a chariot.
4. A river scene where the player follows ducks from a boat.
5. Deer crossing a green hill.
6. Deer pursued from a chariot/vehicle.

The published control is swipe on the bow to pull, rotate, and aim; releasing shoots the arrow. At the beginning of a level, the required number of birds or ducks is shown. Completing the target unlocks the next level, and coins can also be used to unlock another level. The listing describes a shop for hunting equipment and a running horse that can be tapped for gifts including coins, arrows, and a special burning-arrow power-up.
